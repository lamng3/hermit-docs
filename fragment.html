hermitpy.load

Fragment

The loader turns an rdflib graph into a TBox and an ABox. A construct outside the fragment raises UnsupportedConstruct. It is not dropped.

Accepted

ConstructWhat the loader does
Named classes, owl:Thing, owl:NothingNamed IRIs other than Thing and Nothing are recorded as classes to classify.
rdfs:subClassOfA named class on the left unfolds into its superclass. A complex left-hand side becomes a general inclusion.
owl:equivalentClassTwo inclusions, one each way. Equivalence of two properties is refused.
owl:disjointWithEach class is a subclass of the complement of the other. Disjoint properties are refused.
owl:complementOf, owl:intersectionOf, owl:unionOfParsed and pushed into negation normal form.
owl:someValuesFrom, owl:allValuesFromExistential and universal restrictions on object properties, including inverse roles written with owl:inverseOf.
owl:inverseOfNamed inverses are recorded both ways. A property declared inverse of itself is treated as symmetric.
owl:TransitiveProperty, owl:SymmetricPropertyNamed properties only. Transitivity is copied onto a named inverse.
rdfs:domain, rdfs:rangeObject-property domain and range, applied to a role and to its inverse. A datatype-property domain is an existential over rdfs:Literal.
Class assertionsindividual a Class on a named individual.
Object-property assertionsTwo named individuals and a named property.
Literal assertionsA named individual, a property, and a supported literal.

Source: load.py.

Datatypes

Restrictions may use owl:onDatatype and owl:withRestrictions on a datatype property in the forward direction. The facets are xsd:minInclusive, xsd:maxInclusive, xsd:minExclusive, and xsd:maxExclusive. A datatype restriction on an inverse property is refused.

The numeric family is xsd:integer, xsd:int, xsd:long, xsd:short, xsd:nonNegativeInteger, xsd:positiveInteger, xsd:nonPositiveInteger, xsd:negativeInteger, xsd:decimal, xsd:double, xsd:float, owl:real, and owl:rational. Integer datatypes stay on integers when bounds are tightened. The other families are xsd:string, xsd:anyURI, xsd:date, xsd:dateTime, xsd:boolean, and rdfs:Literal. Any other datatype raises UnsupportedConstruct.

An existential numeric bound and a universal numeric bound that cannot overlap make the class unsatisfiable. The ontology can still be consistent.

Refused

These raise UnsupportedConstruct. The exception names the construct. See Errors.

  • Cardinality, including owl:maxCardinality, owl:minCardinality, owl:qualifiedCardinality, and the exact forms.
  • Property chains, role inclusion (rdfs:subPropertyOf), owl:equivalentProperty, and owl:propertyDisjointWith.
  • Nominals (owl:oneOf), keys, owl:hasSelf, and owl:disjointUnionOf.
  • owl:sameAs, owl:differentFrom, owl:AllDifferent, owl:AllDisjointClasses, and owl:AllDisjointProperties.
  • owl:FunctionalProperty, owl:InverseFunctionalProperty, owl:AsymmetricProperty, owl:IrreflexiveProperty, and owl:ReflexiveProperty.
  • Negative property assertions, SWRL, and owl:imports.
  • Anonymous individuals, anonymous transitive or symmetric properties, cyclic class expressions, and a property with two conflicting owl:inverseOf targets.

Any OWL predicate outside the accepted set is refused the same way, so a new constructor is not silently skipped.

Ignored

Annotation triples are skipped: rdfs:label, rdfs:comment, rdfs:seeAlso, rdfs:isDefinedBy, owl:versionInfo, owl:deprecated, and any property typed owl:AnnotationProperty. Ontology header triples such as owl:versionIRI, owl:priorVersion, owl:backwardCompatibleWith, and owl:incompatibleWith are not axioms.

How axioms land

A subclass axiom whose left side is a named class is stored as an unfolding of that name. Unfolding is applied when the tableau sees the atom. A left side that is a conjunction, a restriction, or another complex concept is stored as a general concept inclusion and added to every node.

owl:Nothing on the left of an inclusion is dropped. A superclass of owl:Thing is dropped. Declarations of owl:Class, owl:ObjectProperty, owl:DatatypeProperty, owl:NamedIndividual, and owl:Ontology are not class assertions.
